本版在 studio-v2.62.0 之后：新建会话不再先弹「选择项目」，点下直接进窗口、落在「任务」组，归属交给输入框上方的项目药丸；任务会话按日期归档，首轮发言后向标题模型要名字；FuchaZ 的一批界面修剪移植进来；版本页重新挂上 QQ 群；仓库更名为 DeepSeek-Reasonix-torch。

## 新增

- **新建会话直接进会话窗口**：不再先弹选择框，新对话落在内核自己的工作区，进侧栏的「任务」组 (dc0f9a3)
- **输入框上方的项目药丸**：新对话的引导——列出记住的项目（搜索、当前标记）、新建空白项目（起名+选位置，浏览器端没有选择器时回退手输路径）、使用现有文件夹、不使用项目；对话一旦开始（或已放置）药丸就退场 (eb03e49)
- **任务会话按日期归档**：任务会话的记录落在 `…/sessions/2026/11/14/` 这样的日期目录里，像日历一样翻；项目会话保持平铺 (5bd7fe1)
- **首轮发言自动命名**：第一轮发出时就向标题模型要一个名字，回答还在生成，侧栏读到的已经是真名字；标题模型由 `agent.title_model` 指定，没配就不自动起名 (6be5a6b)
- **仪表栏统计「对话轮次」**：只数你发起的轮——发言中途的插话和折叠摘要不算，从记录重新推导 (6be5a6b)
- **本机终端前端的网页端可以添加模型源**：授权跟随监听地址——loopback 才放行写入，后开的窗格同样继承 (086c80e)
- 版本页重新挂上 QQ 群：群号 1821170167 直接显示，README 两个语言版也带上 (7bec0c2)
- 右侧控制台的启动器撑满自己所在的列，不再被 420px 一类的上限裁着；记忆正文与编辑器同样撑满 (01279b6)

## 变更

- 会话树分成「项目」「任务」两组：分组标题是纯文字——「项目」「任务」，点按即折叠本组；任务组的会话直接挂在「任务」标题下，不再有中间的文件夹行 (8222768)
- 项目行带着文件夹图标：折角 + 文件夹 + 名字；任务组的会话行无图标 (3f8d3f6)
- 窗口栏对任务会话只显示会话标题——不属于任何项目的对话不再借「auto」占一个位置 (fd1f5da)
- 「任务列表」是标签不是按钮：悬停不再出现指针与可点击的错觉 (fa9e491)
- 范围菜单从点击处向右下展开，宽度按最长的行收：右侧一列是数字，最近的方向箭头跟着「最近」两个字走 (eb03e49)
- dock 与侧栏的滑出动画换成更柔和的缓动；思考卡的「实时」模式与「展开」一样不限窗口高度；思考文字有自己的字重，轻体模式下更轻 (01279b6)
- 设置行的分段控件对齐行的边缘；写锁的搜索结果落到承载它的会话页；权限与安全›扩展范围的项目列表挂到 body 上，不再被设置页的滚动容器裁切 (01279b6)
- 仓库与介绍更名 DeepSeek-Reasonix-torch，README 两个语言版按新名字重写 (53d81d4)

## 修复

- 右侧控制台的拖拽分栏条在新的网格布局下保持整列高度——此前它塌成一条看不见的缝，右栏完全拉不动 (fd1f5da)
- 契约测试跟随新的落地契约：记住的项目不再接住新会话，重排记住列表也不再决定默认落地处 (e84621f)

## 移除

- 先打开一个项目的引导页：归属由输入框上方的项目药丸回答，空会话只自我介绍 (eb03e49)

## 升级须知

- 任务会话从此写入日期目录；旧的任务会话文件仍在原处，侧栏照常列出它们 (5bd7fe1)
- 自动命名需要 `agent.title_model` 指向一个模型，没配就不自动起名 (6be5a6b)
- `agent.write_lease` 的搜索结果指向会话页；`desktop.update_mode` 的搜索指向维持原处 (086c80e)

<!-- lang:en -->

This one follows studio-v2.62.0: new conversations open straight into the window,
the composer's project pill decides where they belong, task conversations file by
date, the first turn asks a title model, FuchaZ trims land, and the repo is now
DeepSeek-Reasonix-torch.

## New

- A new conversation opens straight into the window and lands in the 任务 group; the
  project pill above the composer decides where it belongs (dc0f9a3)
- The project pill is the fresh conversation's guide: remembered projects (search,
  current mark), a blank project (name + folder, hand-typed path when the browser has no
  picker), an existing folder, or 不使用项目; once placed or once the conversation has
  content, the pill leaves (eb03e49)
- Task conversations file themselves by date (…/2026/11/14/); project sessions stay
  flat (5bd7fe1)
- The first turn asks the title model while it runs, so the sidebar reads a real name;
  `agent.title_model` gates it — none, no unprompted naming (6be5a6b)
- The meter rail counts the turns the person authored (6be5a6b) — steers and fold
  digests do not
- A loopback terminal frontend can add a model source; later panes inherit the
  grant (086c80e)
- The version page carries the QQ group again: 1821170167 shows right there, and both
  READMEs say it (7bec0c2)
- The workbench launcher fills its column; the memory editors fill theirs (01279b6)

## Changed

- The session tree's two groups 项目 / 任务 are plain labels that fold their group;
  task conversations hang directly under 任务 with no folder row (8222768)
- The project row carries its folder icon again; task rows go unmarked (3f8d3f6)
- The window bar names a task conversation by its title alone (fd1f5da)
- 任务列表 is a label, not a button that only looked clickable (fa9e491)
- The scope menu opens at the click toward the bottom-right, sized to its longest row;
  the arrow rides the 最近 label and the count column stays numbers only (eb03e49)
- The dock and sidebar slides ease gently; a live thought uses the whole window; the
  thought text carries its own weight token (01279b6)
- The settings rows' segmented control lands at the row's edge; the write-lease search
  result moved to the session page that hosts it; the 扩展范围 pill portals its project
  list out of the settings scroll box (01279b6)
- The repo and its intro are renamed DeepSeek-Reasonix-torch; both READMEs are
  rewritten around the new name (53d81d4)

## Fixed

- The right console's drag divider keeps its full column height under the new grid —
  before, it collapsed to an invisible sliver and the sidebar would not resize
  (fd1f5da)
- The contract tests follow the new landing: a remembered folder no longer catches a
  fresh conversation, and reordering the list no longer decides the default (e84621f)

## Removed

- The 先打开一个项目 onboarding hero: the pill above the composer answers where a
  conversation belongs, and an empty session just introduces itself (eb03e49)

## Upgrade notes

- Task conversations write into date folders; older files stay where they are and the
  sidebar still lists them (5bd7fe1)
- Auto-naming needs `agent.title_model` pointed at a model (6be5a6b)
- `agent.write_lease`'s search results point at the session page;
  `desktop.update_mode`'s stay where they were (086c80e)
