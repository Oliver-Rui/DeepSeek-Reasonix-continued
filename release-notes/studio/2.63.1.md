本版收 2.63.0 的一批尾巴：任务会话按日期归档后，删除、归档、导出与批量删除被「该路径不属于任何已知的工作区」挡住，现在边界认到日期目录；队列找回自己的上限，不再把转录与输入框挤出窗口；错误提示浮到计划卡之上；计划卡与对话卡对齐；批量选择以 X 收尾；新建空白项目的选择器不再落进应用自己的内部目录。

## 修复

- **日期归档的任务会话删不掉**：删除、归档、导出与批量删除被「该路径不属于任何已知的工作区」拒绝——归属判定覆盖会话树下的日期目录，扁平会话行为不变 (2cf2179)
- **删除项目连带报错**：树里指向别处日期目录的会话一并放行，项目删除不再被它们挡住 (2cf2179)
- **输入框上下的东西把转录和输入框挤出窗口**：队列行区找回上限并随窗口折档收缩，浮动的运行通知与错误条同受预算约束 (49b28bf)
- **被截断的队列预览看不了全文**：悬停半秒弹出全文浮层，过长自己滚——预览浮层回到了队列里 (49b28bf)
- **错误提示被计划卡盖住**：通知栈浮到计划卡之上，报错一定看得见 (49b28bf)
- **计划卡与对话卡没对齐**：计划卡左右边缘与对话卡重合 (49b28bf)
- **批量选择条**：「完成选择」按钮改为 X，语义不变 (01fc659)
- **新建空白项目的源文件夹**：选择器从平台自己的上次目录打开，不再落进应用内部的工作区目录 (01fc659)

2.63.0's loose ends: dated task conversations refused to delete, archive or
export as outside any known workspace — the boundary reaches into the dated
directories; the queue finds its ceiling again; the notices rise above the plan
card; the batch bar ends with an X; the picker starts where the platform last
used it.

## Fixed

- **A dated task conversation could not be deleted**: removal, archiving,
  export and batch removal all answered the outside-any-workspace refusal —
  ownership now covers the dated directories under the sessions tree (2cf2179)
- **Deleting a project dragged refusals along**: conversations whose files sit
  in another workspace's dated directories no longer block it (2cf2179)
- **The queue pushed the transcript and the box off the window**: the row list
  has its ceiling back and folds with the window, and the floating run notices
  and error bars answer to the same budget (49b28bf)
- **A cut queue preview could not be read in full**: hovering half a second
  opens the full-text overlay, which scrolls past its own ceiling — the preview
  overlay is back in the queue (49b28bf)
- **Error notices hid behind the plan card**: the notice stack rises above it,
  so a refusal is always readable (49b28bf)
- **The plan card missed the composer's edges**: its left and right edges now
  meet the composer card's (49b28bf)
- **The batch bar**: 「完成选择」 became an X, same meaning (01fc659)
- **The blank project's source folder**: the picker starts at the platform's
  own last-used folder instead of inside the app's internal workspace (01fc659)
